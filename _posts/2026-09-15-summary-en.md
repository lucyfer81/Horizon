## [Laurie Voss: AI Collapses Code Cost, Making Product Engineering the Core of Software Work](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/14/laurie-voss/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Laurie Voss, a prominent figure in the software industry, argues that generative AI has drastically reduced the cost of writing code, and the costs of reviewing, fixing, and operating it are following suit. He posits that the remaining, non-transferable work of software creation is defining what users want and crafting a pleasant experience, which will constitute the entirety of the job as software demand grows infinitely. This perspective signals a fundamental paradigm shift in software engineering, where the economic value moves from code implementation to product discovery and user-centric design. It implies that future software professionals will need to prioritize skills in product definition, UX/UI design, and understanding user needs over pure coding prowess. Voss&\#x27;s argument hinges on the assumption that AI-driven cost reductions for code maintenance and operations will continue to match the collapse in initial writing costs. He also highlights a key economic principle: the cost of product definition and UX design is &\#x27;per piece of software and doesn&\#x27;t transfer,&\#x27; making it scale with demand rather than being automated away.
