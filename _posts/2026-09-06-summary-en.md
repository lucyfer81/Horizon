## [Private German rocket RFA One reaches orbit from European soil for the first time.](https://www.space.com/space-exploration/launches-spacecraft/isar-aerospace-second-launch-norway-andoya-spaceport-spectrum-rocket) ⭐️ 7.0/10

The RFA One rocket, developed by the German private company Rocket Factory Augsburg \(RFA\), successfully reached orbit from the Andøya Spaceport in Norway. This marks the first time a privately developed German rocket has achieved orbit from European soil. This success is a historic milestone for Europe&\#x27;s independent space capabilities, demonstrating the viability of its private launch sector. It reduces reliance on foreign launch providers and strengthens Europe&\#x27;s position in the competitive small satellite launch market, which is projected to grow significantly. The RFA One is a three-stage, small-lift launch vehicle capable of carrying payloads of up to 1,300 kg to low-Earth orbit. The launch originated from Andøya Spaceport in Norway, which is a European facility, distinguishing this achievement from previous launches from non-European sites like French Guiana.

## [Research Paper Proposes LLMs as a &\#x27;Cognitive Virus&\#x27;](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03344) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A research paper published on arXiv provocatively frames Large Language Models \(LLMs\) as a form of &\#x27;cognitive virus,&\#x27; examining their potential to alter human thought, memory, and information transmission. The paper draws parallels between the self-propagating nature of computer viruses and the way ideas or cultural elements spread, applying this metaphor to LLMs. This conceptual framing matters because it provides a novel lens for understanding the profound, systemic impact of AI on society, moving beyond technical performance to consider how these tools might reshape cognition and culture. It encourages critical reflection on the long-term consequences of outsourcing cognitive tasks and memory to AI systems within sociotechnical systems. The paper explicitly links its argument to established fields like evolutionary memetics and the concept of &\#x27;viruses of the mind,&\#x27; positioning LLMs as a modern, technological instantiation of these ideas. A key caveat is that the &\#x27;virus&\#x27; metaphor is intentionally provocative and not a literal biological claim, aiming to stimulate debate about AI&\#x27;s role in cultural evolution.

**Background**: The concept of a &\#x27;cognitive virus&\#x27; or &\#x27;virus of the mind&\#x27; stems from memetics, a theory of cultural evolution where ideas \(memes\) replicate and evolve similarly to genes. Sociotechnical systems theory studies the complex interplay between social structures and technology, recognizing that neither can be fully understood in isolation. Research is actively exploring parallels and differences between human memory mechanisms and the &\#x27;memory&\#x27; architectures used in LLMs.
