## [Essay Argues Future Programming is &\#x27;Yes, And&\#x27; Collaboration with AI](https://htmx.org/essays/yes-and/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

An essay published on htmx.org argues that the role of the programmer will evolve from direct coding to a &\#x27;yes, and&\#x27; style of collaboration with AI. The author contends that human skills in design, testing, and providing meticulous guidance will remain essential, even as AI becomes a more capable coding partner. This perspective is crucial for software engineers and students considering the field, as it reframes the AI threat narrative into one of partnership and skill evolution. It suggests that future success will depend less on raw coding ability and more on high-level architectural thinking, problem decomposition, and the ability to guide and verify AI-generated outputs. The essay specifically draws an analogy to the shift from assembly to high-level languages but notes a key difference: compilers are deterministic, while current AI tools are not, making human reasoning and verification critical. The author wrote the piece partly to advise students, including his own son who is studying computer science.

In AI development, frameworks like Microsoft&\#x27;s AutoGen are designed for multi-agent collaboration, and the concept of &\#x27;human-in-the-loop&\#x27; \(HITL\) describes systems where human oversight is integral to the AI&\#x27;s operation. The practice of &\#x27;AI pair programming&\#x27; or &\#x27;vibe coding&\#x27; is an emerging model where a developer guides an AI agent to generate and refine code.

## [Whistle: A 16.9 MB Speech-to-Text Model for Local, Efficient Transcription](https://cactuscompute.com/blog/whistle) ⭐️ 7.0/10

The Whistle model, a compact speech-to-text \(STT\) system with a size of only 16.9 MB, has been released, enabling efficient local transcription without relying on cloud services. This model is specifically designed for deployment on resource-constrained edge and embedded devices. This matters because it represents a significant step towards practical, privacy-preserving, and low-latency speech recognition on devices like smart home assistants, IoT gadgets, and offline applications. It challenges the dominance of large, cloud-dependent models by offering a viable, efficient alternative for the growing edge AI and embedded systems market. While extremely compact, the model&\#x27;s accuracy may be lower compared to larger alternatives, as indicated by a user&\#x27;s comparison where it correctly transcribed 70 out of 170 messages versus a larger model&\#x27;s 168. A notable limitation mentioned in community discussion is the lack of real-time streaming transcription output, which is considered essential for many live STT applications.

hackernews · gmays · Oct 8, 16:59 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50008427)

**Background**: Speech-to-text \(STT\) models convert spoken language into written text. Traditionally, high-quality STT required powerful cloud servers, but there is a growing trend towards &\#x27;edge AI&\#x27; where models run locally on devices for lower latency, better privacy, and offline functionality. Compact models like Whistle are part of the TinyML movement, which focuses on deploying machine learning on microcontrollers and other resource-limited embedded systems.

## [2025 research proposes ADHD as a circadian rhythm disorder, exploring chronotherapy implications.](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychiatry/articles/10.3389/fpsyt.2025.1697900/full) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A 2025 research article published in Frontiers in Psychiatry synthesizes evidence to propose that Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder \(ADHD\) may be fundamentally a circadian rhythm disorder. The article explores the implications of this hypothesis for chronotherapeutic treatments, such as bright light therapy and melatonin administration. This hypothesis could fundamentally reframe the understanding and treatment of ADHD, shifting focus towards regulating sleep-wake cycles and light exposure. If validated, it could lead to new, non-pharmacological treatment avenues and improve outcomes for millions of individuals with ADHD who also commonly experience sleep disturbances. The article notes that the causality between circadian rhythms and ADHD is likely bidirectional, meaning ADHD symptoms can disrupt sleep patterns, and circadian disruptions can exacerbate ADHD. A key limitation is that the journal Frontiers in Psychiatry is considered by some in the scientific community to be a lower-quality, rapid-publication outlet, which necessitates cautious interpretation of its findings.

hackernews · bookofjoe · Oct 8, 20:42 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50011928)

**Background**: Circadian rhythms are the body&\#x27;s approximately 24-hour internal clocks that regulate sleep, hormone release, and other physiological processes. A circadian rhythm disorder occurs when this internal clock is misaligned with the external environment, leading to problems like insomnia or excessive daytime sleepiness. Chronotherapy is a behavioral treatment that aims to reset these rhythms by systematically adjusting sleep times or using light and melatonin to align the internal clock with a desired schedule.
